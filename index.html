Dear Meswa. ♡

Happy National Girlfriend Day!
Thank you for choosing me—
not just as your lover, but as your friend.
You are one of the most beautiful parts of my life,
a light I never knew I needed until I found you.

I pray for you every day—
that your dreams come true,
and your fears fade like morning mist.

Through calm and chaos, joy and doubt,
I’ll be here—always by your side.

The words “I love you” fall short
of the depth I feel inside.
Because only you can define what love means to me—
you are the reason, the meaning, the answer.

I love you, now and always. ♡ ♡ ♡
